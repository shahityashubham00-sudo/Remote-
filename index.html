📡 Cast your phone screen

A web page can’t start screen casting by itself, so this gets the TV ready and walks you through it on your phone.

  1. Android: swipe down twice from the top, tap Smart View / Cast / Screen cast, then pick your Bravia.
  2. iPhone: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, then pick your TV (needs AirPlay support on your model).
  3. Bravia TVs with Google TV have Chromecast built in, so they are always ready to receive a cast.
